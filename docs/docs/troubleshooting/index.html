Troubleshooting and updating

Fix the common problems, and what to expect when Textem updates.

On this page

  • “Can't reach Google Messages”
  • The window is blank or the inbox never appears
  • “Trying to reach your phone”
  • A message says “View on your phone”
  • Disconnected after using Messages in a browser
  • Sign-in verification fails
  • No notifications
  • Updating

“Can't reach Google Messages”

If the page fails to load (offline, after sleep, a network blip), Textem retries on its own after 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 seconds and shows the reason. Click Try Again or press ⇧⌘R.

The window is blank or the inbox never appears

If the page loads but your conversations never appear, Textem reloads it once after about 40 seconds. If they still don't appear, it shows the Try Again screen. Quitting and reopening Textem also fixes it.

“Trying to reach your phone”

Google Messages on your phone needs to be on and connected. Opening the Messages app on your phone usually fixes it.

A message says “View on your phone”

Google sends some messages, usually business messages with rich cards, images or buttons, only to your phone. Google Messages for web never receives them, so Textem can't show them. Open them on your phone.

Disconnected after using Messages in a browser

Only one computer can be active per account. Press ⇧⌘R in Textem to make it the active one again.

Sign-in verification fails

You probably chose a passkey. Sign in again, choose Enter your password, and pick a non-passkey second step. See Sign in and pair.

No notifications

  1. Check System Settings > Notifications > Textem allows alerts.
  2. Check Mute notifications is off in Textem's Settings and in the menu-bar menu.
  3. Notifications appear when Textem isn't the frontmost app.
  4. Choose Help > Diagnostics > Send Test Notification to test delivery on its own.

Updating

Textem checks for updates once a day while it's open and asks before installing. Choose Textem > Check for Updates… to check now.

Coming from 1.0b1? Textem's app identity changed in 1.0b2, so that one update has to be downloaded by hand. Your settings and Google sign-in carry over. Afterwards, Textem shows a short sheet, once, to turn notifications, Textem Codes and launch at login back on. You can open it again with Help > After Updating….
