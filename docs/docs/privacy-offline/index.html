Privacy options and Offline Mode

Hide what's on screen, know what Textem connects to, and browse saved content offline.

On this page

  • Privacy options
  • What Textem connects to
  • Offline Mode Experimental

Privacy options

In Textem > Settings…:

  • Privacy > Blur message previews: previews and messages are blurred until you hover over them.
  • Privacy > Hide avatars: removes contact pictures.
  • Notifications > Hide sender and message preview: banners say only "New message".

These change only what's shown on your Mac. Read receipts and what you send are unaffected.

What Textem connects to

Textem shows Google's own Messages for web and talks to Google. It adds no analytics, telemetry, accounts or relay servers, and your session is stored on your Mac. The only other connection is the daily update check to textem.scarcrux.com, which you can turn off in Settings > Updates. To keep Google's sign-in working inside an app, Textem turns off WebKit's cross-site tracking prevention for its own Google session; this doesn't affect Safari.

Offline Mode Experimental

View > Offline Mode pauses Google Messages and shows what Textem has saved on this Mac. Choose Go Online to return. The choice is remembered across restarts.

Today it shows phone history and pictures saved through Textem's developer Android companion; without the companion it's empty. Ordinary conversations aren't stored offline.
