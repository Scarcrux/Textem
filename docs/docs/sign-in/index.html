Sign in and pair with your phone

Connect Textem to Google Messages on your phone. You only do this once.

On this page

  • Don't use a passkey
  • Pair
  • One computer at a time

Don't use a passkey

Passkeys can't work inside Textem. Apple only lets an app use passkeys for websites it owns, and Textem doesn't own google.com. When Google asks how to sign in, choose Enter your password, and pick a non-passkey second step.

Pair

Textem shows these steps the first time it opens. You can see them again any time with Help > Sign-in Help….

  1. On your phone, open Google Messages, tap your account menu (top right), then Device pairing.
  2. In Textem, sign in with the same Google account and choose Enter your password.
  3. For the second step, choose a Google prompt ("Tap Yes"), an SMS or email code, or an authenticator app.
  4. Your phone shows three emoji. Tap the one that matches what Textem shows.

Textem then stays signed in across restarts.

One computer at a time

Google allows one active computer per account. If you open Messages for web in a browser or on another computer, that one becomes active and Textem disconnects. To switch back, press ⇧⌘R (View > Reload from Origin).
